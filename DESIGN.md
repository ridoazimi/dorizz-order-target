# Direction
User brief: simple Shopee-like internal order dashboard, focus on incoming order count.
Energy 2 / Rhythm 2 / Motion 1.
Warm white working surface and charcoal numerals keep a large total legible on phones and office displays. Rust orange identifies target progress, not decoration. Tabular system numerals avoid digit reflow. One large count, a thin progress rule, a compact summary strip, then a daily chart. No sidebar, fictional metrics, logos, marketing blocks or decorative icons. Motion only signals newly received server data; reduced-motion disables it.
